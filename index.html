The whole thing

Three steps, then it just works.

No app to install on either side. The card holds a secure link; the phone reads it the moment they touch.

STEP 01

Tap

Hold the card to the top of any modern phone. NFC wakes up and a prompt slides in — nothing to download.

STEP 02

Load

Your profile opens in the browser: name, role, links, socials, a save-to-contacts button, whatever you've set.

STEP 03

Connect

They save you in a second. Change your details anytime from your dashboard — the card never needs reprinting.
