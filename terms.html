What we collect

When you share your details after tapping a Tapliy card, we collect the information you enter: your name, and optionally your phone number and email address.

Why we collect it

  • To let the card owner receive your contact details directly, which is the whole point of tapping the card.
  • If you tick the separate marketing checkbox, to send you promotional messages or offers (such as a discount code) from Tapliy and/or the card owner.

We do not use your details for any other purpose, and we do not sell your data.

Where it's stored

Your details are stored securely in our database (Supabase) and are only visible to the card owner you shared them with and Tapliy staff for support and administration.

Your rights

Under the PDPA, you can ask to access, correct, or withdraw consent for your personal data at any time. To do this, contact the card owner directly, or email privacy@tapd.sg and we'll action your request.

Retention

We keep your details for as long as your relationship with the card owner is active, or until you ask us to delete them.
